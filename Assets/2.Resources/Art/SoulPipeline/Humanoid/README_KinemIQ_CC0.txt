KinemIQ Free Animation Pack (CC0) - 35 game animations
=======================================================

35 character animations for games, each as FBX and GLB (skeleton + animation), on one humanoid skeleton
(the Quaternius Universal skeleton, 66 bones), 30 fps. KinemIQ_Mannequin.fbx / .glb = the same skeleton
with the mannequin mesh (an idle) - use it as the character, the clips play on it.

Idles, walks, runs, sprint, crouch, zombie walk and scratch, jump (take-off, air, landing),
roll, punches (jab, cross, hook), hit reactions, a death, get up, sit down / stand up,
a dance, climb, interact, pick up, sword slash / idle / block.

Unity: import KinemIQ_Mannequin.fbx and the clips, Rig = Humanoid (clips: Copy From Other Avatar). Unreal Engine, Blender and Godot: FBX or GLB.
